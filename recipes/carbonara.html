Pasta alla Carbonara

Photo Credit: Luca Nebuloni

Description

A couple of paragraphs about carbonara here.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound guanciale (cured pork cheek), diced
  • 1 (16 ounce) package bucatini
  • 3 large eggs
  • 10 tablespoons grated Pecorino Romano cheese
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat; add guanciale. Cook, turning occasionally, until evenly browned and crispy, 5 to 10 minutes. Remove from heat and drain on paper towels.
  2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook bucatini in the boiling water, stirring occasionally. When the pasta becomes tender yet firm to the bite, or about 2 minutes before the time listed on the package instructions. Drain and return to the pot. Let cool, stirring occasionally, about 5 minutes.
  3. Whisk eggs, 1/2 of the Pecorino Romano cheese, and some black pepper in a bowl until smooth and creamy.
  4. Pour egg mixture over pasta, stirring quickly, until creamy and slightly cooled. Stir in guanciale.
  5. Top with remaining Pecorino Romano cheese and more black pepper.
